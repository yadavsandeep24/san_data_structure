# Appendix G — 12-Day DSA Plan Map (120 Problems)

A popular 12-day plan lists 120 interview problems ([Ishant, Medium](https://medium.com/@ishant.7123217/12-day-dsa-preparation-plan-120-coding-problems-to-practice-before-your-interviews-ce436a6a747f)). This appendix maps every one to where it is solved in this book, so you can follow the plan day by day.

- **67 problems** were missing and now live in **Part X** ([Ch 32](../part-x/32-arrays-hashing-matrix.md)–[Ch 37](../part-x/37-graphs-dynamic-programming.md)).
- The rest were already covered in topic chapters, the [Ch 24 question bank](../part-vii/24-interview-questions.md), the [Ch 27 hard bank](../part-viii/27-hard-drill-bank.md) or the chapter solutions.
- The plan repeats a few problems on two days (marked *repeat*); they link to the same solution.

**How to use:** one day per sitting; for each problem, say the brute force and the better idea out loud before opening the link; time-box 20–25 minutes for mediums.
